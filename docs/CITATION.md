# Citing the formalization

A sentence for the paper is

```latex
A Lean~4 formalization of \Cref{thm:classification} is
available in \cite{MarquesLeanII}.
```

The [proof revision](https://github.com/DiegoMarquesMath/MahlerLeanII/tree/668b542bbd5bb582e0dbf534e71375026c52b5fd) and its
[successful verification](https://github.com/DiegoMarquesMath/MahlerLeanII/actions/runs/41273103486) identify the archived statement.
The following entry cites that exact revision.

```latex
\bibitem{MarquesLeanII}
D.~Marques, \emph{MahlerLeanII: Lean~4 formalization of the rational
classification in Mahler's problem on Liouville numbers},
commit~\texttt{668b542bbd5b},
\url{https://github.com/DiegoMarquesMath/MahlerLeanII/tree/668b542bbd5bb582e0dbf534e71375026c52b5fd}.
```
