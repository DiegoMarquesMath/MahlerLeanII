# Proof scope beyond Theorem 1.2

The [manuscript correspondence](STATUS.md) identifies all three implications
of the rational classification and the results used to prove them. Further
formalization can proceed independently on the following questions.

- In Lemma 4.1, turn the reconstructed coefficient vector into the perturbed
  monic pair and prove pole exclusion on the whole interval.
- In Proposition 6.1 and Theorem 1.3, formalize the full binary tree, its
  Cantor set, and the common irrationality-exponent bound on the images.
- Derive the exact-preservation, analytic and entire corollaries, with the
  appropriate rational fiber and pole conditions.
- Formalize the size results for the coefficient field, the fixed-gap
  example and the limits on a uniform escape exponent.
